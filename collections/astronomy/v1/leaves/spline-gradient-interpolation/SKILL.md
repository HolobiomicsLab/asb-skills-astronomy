# spline-gradient-interpolation

## Summary

Fits cubic UnivariateSpline interpolants (k=3, s=0.005, ext=0) of log10 surface density, log10 temperature, and log10 pressure against log10 radius from a solved pAGN disk, and differentiates them to obtain the local gradients (dSigmadR, dTdR, dPdR) needed by the Grishin et al. 2023 migration-torque coefficient formulas.

## When to use

Use when migration-torque coefficients (Jiménez & Masset 2017, Masset 2017, Paardekooper et al. 2010 via Grishin et al. 2023) must be evaluated on a solved pAGN Sirko & Goodman or Thompson disk and the local surface-density, thermal, and pressure gradients are required at each orbital radius.

## When NOT to use

- The disk has not been solved yet — the splines must be fit to solve_disk(N=1e4) output profiles, not raw model parameters
- The disk solution is generated by a tool other than pAGN's Sirko & Goodman or Thompson models, where the Sigma = 2*rho*h convention and log10-radius spline settings may not apply
- A general-purpose smoothing task unrelated to AGN-disk gradient computation, where a different k or s would be more appropriate

## Inputs

- Solved pAGN disk object (SirkoAGN or ThompsonAGN) with 1D radial profiles from solve_disk(N=1e4): radius, Omega, temperature, density, scale height, sound speed
- Surface density derived as Sigma = 2*rho*h
- scipy >= 1.10.0 environment

## Outputs

- UnivariateSpline objects (k=3, s=0.005, ext=0) of log10(Sigma), log10(T), log10(P) versus log10(R), and their derivative functions dSigmadR, dTdR, dPdR
- Torque coefficients CI_jm17_iso, CI_jm17_tot, CI_p10, CL evaluated at each grid radius, assembled into torque_coefficients.csv with columns radius, surface density, temperature, and the four coefficient columns

## How to apply

First solve the AGN disk with Sirko.SirkoAGN or Thompson.ThompsonAGN and solve_disk(N=1e4) to obtain radial profiles of radius, temperature, density, and scale height; form Sigma = 2*rho*h. Take log10 of radius and of Sigma, T, and P, then fit scipy.interpolate.UnivariateSpline(k=3, s=0.005, ext=0) over log10(R) for each — this requires scipy version 1.10.0 or later. Differentiate the spline objects analytically (spline.derivative()) to obtain continuous dSigmadR, dTdR, and dPdR functions that can be evaluated at the migrator's orbital radius (e.g. a 50 solar-mass BH with q=5e-6 around a 1e6 solar-mass SMBH). The smoothing parameter s=0.005 suppresses grid-scale noise in the 1e4-point disk solution while preserving the large-scale radial gradients. The resulting gamma expressions (adiabatic, isothermal, thermal) combine these gradients and feed the CI_jm17_iso, CI_jm17_tot, CI_p10, and CL coefficient prescriptions.

## Related tools

- **SciPy** (Provides UnivariateSpline (k=3, s=0.005, ext=0) used to interpolate and differentiate the log-radius disk gradients; version 1.10.0 or later is required)
- **pagn** (Generates the solved Sirko & Goodman / Thompson disk profiles (Sigma, T, P versus R) to which the splines are fit) — https://gitlab.com/aatrani/pagn
- **numpy** (Computes log10 transforms of the radial grid and disk profiles and assembles/saves the coefficient table with numpy.savetxt)

## Examples

```
from scipy.interpolate import UnivariateSpline; Sigmalog10_spline = UnivariateSpline(rlog10, Sigmalog10, k=3, s=0.005, ext=0)
```

- torque_coefficients.csv contains finite torque-coefficient values across the full radial grid, with a radius column plus all four coefficient columns
- For the Thompson use case, the accretion-rate diagnostic shows Mdot_out below the critical rate Mdot_c at r = Rout before gradients are used
- The Sirko & Goodman solution fed to the splines shows temperature decreasing outward from ~1e5-1e6 K and h/r below one in the inner disk regions
- SciPy version is 1.10.0 or later and the spline call uses k=3, s=0.005, ext=0

## Limitations

- Requires scipy version 1.10.0 or later for the UnivariateSpline behaviour used
- ext=0 allows extrapolation outside the fitted radius range, where gradients may be unreliable; gradients should be evaluated within the solved disk's radial grid
- The smoothing parameter s=0.005 is tuned to the N=1e4 grid resolution; substantially different grid resolutions may need recalibration
- The GitHub repository DariaGangardt/pAGN is archived and no longer maintained; the project has moved to GitLab
